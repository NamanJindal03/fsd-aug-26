*Steps to Join Doubt Sessions via PW Skills LMS:**
1. Log in to your PW Skills account.
2. Go to **My Courses** or **Dashboard**.
3. Open your enrolled Full Stack Development with AI Course(Doubt Batch) 
4. Navigate to the **Doubt Classes** section.
5. Select the ongoing or upcoming class.
6. Click on **Join Now** when the class starts.
7. If prompted, allow microphone and camera permissions (only if required).
8. You will enter the live session directly through the **PW Skills LMS**.
Please ensure you join the session through the LMS at the scheduled time.
